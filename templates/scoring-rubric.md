# Scoring Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Poor (1-2) | Moderate (3) | Strong (4-5) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Title match | 25% | Little to no title relevance | Partial title keyword overlap | Exact title match or target senior/lead level |
| Skills match | 30% | Missing required core stack | Has some core skills, missing others | Full alignment with required and preferred stack |
| Experience match | 25% | Insufficient domain/system scale | Relevant domain, minor gaps in scale | Proven track record in equivalent problem domain |
| Seniority fit | 20% | Misaligned seniority level | Adjacent seniority level | Matches target Staff/Principal IC level |
